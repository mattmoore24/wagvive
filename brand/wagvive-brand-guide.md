# Wagvive Brand Guide

## Name & Tagline
- **Brand name:** Wagvive
- **Tagline:** "Wellness for every wag"
- **Domain:** wagvive.com

## Positioning
Pet wellness partner focused on comfort, health, and grooming — appealing especially to owners of senior pets and owners who care about hygiene/comfort. Clean, modern, trustworthy. Not generic, not gimmicky.

## Logo Concept
Paw print mark with a heartbeat/pulse line integrated into the palm pad (nods to "vive" — vitality). Lowercase wordmark "wagvive" in a modern geometric sans-serif (Poppins or similar). Flat, single-color icon for simplicity and scalability.

## Voice Pillars
| Pillar | Means | Doesn't mean |
|---|---|---|
| Caring, not cutesy | Warm, genuine concern for the pet's wellbeing | Baby-talk, excessive puns, "fur babies" overload |
| Confident, not clinical | Speak with the assurance of a knowledgeable friend | Cold, jargon-heavy, medical-textbook tone |
| Clear, not salesy | Plain language, real benefits, no hype | Exclamation-point marketing, fake urgency |
| Calm, not chaotic | Steady, reassuring — matches the wellness positioning | Frantic, overstimulating, cluttered copy |

## Tone by Context
- **Product pages:** Benefit-first, sensory but factual. Speak to the pet's comfort, not just the feature.
- **Social/TikTok:** Relaxed and conversational, still no gimmicks — knowledgeable friend, not hype creator.
- **Customer service:** Warm, patient, solution-first. Acknowledge the concern before offering the fix.
- **About/brand copy:** Slightly more emotional register — okay to talk about aging pets, comfort, quality of life — never sappy or manipulative.

## Quick Do/Don't
- Do: "Built for pets who've earned a little extra comfort."
- Don't: "OMG your fur baby will LOVE this!! 🐾🐾"
- Do: name the real benefit (less shedding, better sleep, calmer grooming)
- Don't: vague superlatives ("the BEST product ever")
